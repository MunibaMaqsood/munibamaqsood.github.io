Common questions

How are short answers marked?

You list one or more accepted answers. Matching ignores capital letters and extra spaces, so “islamabad” and “Islamabad” both count.

What happens when the time runs out?

The exam submits automatically and the candidate sees their result straight away.

Can candidates change an answer before submitting?

Yes. They can move back and forth between questions until they submit or the timer ends.

Do I need to install anything?

No. ExamDesk runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer.
